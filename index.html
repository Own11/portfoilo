My story

hey, i'm Diyar.

i'm 14 and i live in Astana, Kazakhstan. i build things for the internet.

it all started with minecraft. i made my own server, installed and tuned other people's plugins, and then tried writing my own. that's how i got into programming.

now i'm the founder of LabAI, an AI tool for teachers. 1 school is already connected and 5 more are in talks.

i'm also the creator of Alerta, AI monitoring for servers. 50 people are on the waitlist.

earlier i also built Catalyse, a service that builds a visual profile of a university from open data, and AimAI, an AI task manager that finds out why you didn't finish a task.

before that i worked as a UI/UX designer at SANACRM.

outside of code i do MMA.
